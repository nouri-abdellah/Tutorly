# Tutorly — Provisional Mathematics Curriculum Inventory

**Target:** Algerian third-year secondary, Experimental Sciences stream  
**Language:** Arabic  
**Status:** User-supplied provisional baseline; not independently verified as an official syllabus.

## 1. Temporary 11-unit inventory

This is a navigation structure only. It does not resolve differences among the three supplied organizations.

### Unit 01 — Functions, limits, continuity and differentiation
- Finite/infinite and one-sided limits; limits at interval endpoints and infinity.
- Limit operations, indeterminate forms, comparison and squeeze theorems.
- Horizontal, vertical and oblique asymptotes; relative position of a curve and asymptote.
- Continuity at a point/on an interval; familiar functions and operations.
- Intermediate Value Theorem; existence/uniqueness of solutions to f(x)=k where justified; bisection.
- Inverse of a continuous monotone function.
- Derivative, tangent, one-sided differentiability, half-tangents, affine approximation and chain rule.
- Monotonicity, extrema, inflection points, concavity/convexity, infinite branches and complete function study.
- Even/odd functions, symmetry centre/axis, relative curve positions and parameter-based graphical discussion.

### Unit 02 — Numerical sequences
- Definitions; arithmetic/geometric sequences, general terms and sums.
- Mathematical induction.
- Monotonicity, boundedness, limits and convergence.
- Monotone bounded sequence theorems, comparison and adjacent sequences.
- Recursive sequences u(n+1)=f(u(n)); auxiliary sequences and graphical interpretation.
- Growth and investment applications.

### Unit 03 — Natural exponential function
- Definition and algebraic/analytical properties of exp(x)=e^x.
- Derivative, monotonicity, limits and comparative growth.
- Functions involving e^x and composite functions e^(u(x)).
- Exponential equations and inequalities.
- General-base exponentials a^x appear in one supplied progression.

### Unit 04 — Natural logarithmic function
- Definition as inverse of the exponential; also described in the supplied material as the antiderivative of 1/x vanishing at 1.
- Algebraic properties, limits, derivative and comparative growth.
- Composite functions ln(u(x)); logarithmic equations and inequalities.
- Decimal/base-a logarithms and real powers appear depending on progression.

### Unit 05 — Comprehensive function study and graphical transformations
- Composition and monotonicity of composite functions.
- Symmetry centre/axis.
- Translation, symmetry, |f(x)| and f(|x|).
- Relative positions of curves; horizontal, oblique and rotational graphical discussions.
- Parameter-based graphical discussion of equations and inequalities.

### Unit 06 — Complex numbers
- Algebraic form, real/imaginary parts, conjugate and modulus.
- Complex-plane representation by point/vector; argument.
- Trigonometric/exponential forms; De Moivre and Euler formulas.
- Quadratic equations over C; higher-degree equations and roots appear in one progression.
- Geometric distances/angles and loci (lines, circles and other curves).
- Translation, homothety, symmetry, rotation and direct similarities using complex notation.
- Roots of complex numbers and roots of unity appear in one progression.

### Unit 07 — Integral calculus
- Antiderivatives and table of familiar antiderivatives.
- Composite forms such as u'·u^n, u'/u and u'·e^u.
- Definite integral and fundamental theorem of calculus.
- Linearity, Chasles relation, comparison and mean value properties.
- Integration by parts.
- Areas between curves, volumes of solids of revolution and mean value.

### Unit 08 — Differential equations
- First-order equations y'=ay+b and y'=ay.
- Second-order equation y''+ay'+by=0 appears in one supplied list.
- Particular solutions from initial conditions.
- Physical applications such as decay and charging/discharging appear in one progression.

### Unit 09 — Probability and statistics
- Counting principles, arrangements/permutations and combinations.
- Probability, conditional probability, independence, total probability and probability trees.
- Random variables and distributions; expectation E(X), variance V(X), standard deviation σ(X).
- Bernoulli trials and binomial distribution.
- Continuous probability (density/distribution functions) appears in one supplied list.
- Bivariate statistics, linear correlation and regression appear in the trimester outline.

### Unit 10 — Geometry in space
- Scalar product, orthogonality and distance.
- Parametric line representation; Cartesian plane equation.
- Relative positions: line/line, plane/plane and line/plane.
- Distance from point to line/plane.
- Sphere equation and intersections with planes/lines.
- Cylinders/cones of revolution and plane sections appear in the trimester outline.

### Unit 11 — Number theory / arithmetic
- Divisors and multiples in Z; Euclidean division.
- Congruences and arithmetic with congruences.
- Prime numbers, Gauss theorem and Fermat's little theorem.
- Remainders of powers.
- Diophantine equations ax+by=c.
